# Overview

## What it is

A user testing platform built for the Filipino tech community. Developers, students, and small teams who've built a web app but don't have real users yet can get it tested by actual people — not friends who'll just say it's fine, but people who'll genuinely try the tasks and report honestly what confused them.

## The problem

Existing user testing platforms (UserTesting, Maze, Userlytics, Lyssna) are priced for enterprise budgets — from ~$99/month up to six figures a year. Teams and individuals in Southeast Asia have separately reported real difficulty recruiting genuine local testers at all, with outside recruitment agencies charging thousands of dollars for a handful of participants. There's a gap between "ask a friend" and "pay enterprise pricing" — this platform is that gap.

## Who it's for

- Students working on capstone or thesis projects who need real feedback before their defense
- Freelance developers and small teams who want a sanity check before client handoff
- Solo builders and indie hackers in the PH tech scene without a personal network of testers to ask

## How it works, in one pass

A poster submits a web app and a task list with a budget. Real Filipino testers pick up available slots, complete the tasks within a set time window, answer structured and open-ended questions, and submit supporting recordings/images. The poster reviews each submission individually and releases payment per tester.

## Why it's different

- Priced for students and freelancers (per-tester rate ₱50–100), not enterprise budgets
- Testers who understand local context — language, apps, payment methods like GCash — instead of a random global panel
- Fast async turnaround instead of scheduled moderated sessions
- Public listing visibility (teaser level) creates organic discovery instead of requiring outbound recruitment
