# Paper Test Guide (Validation Step 1)

This guide outlines the validation framework for the **subukAn** user testing platform before any line of code is written. It contains the concept pitch, structured validation scripts for builders (posters) and testers, and a feedback logging framework to track responses.

---

## 1. The 1-Page Concept Pitch
*Copy and print/share this section directly with validation targets.*

```
   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │                       subukAn                          │
   │      Real User Testing for PH Builders & Testers      │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Hook
**Don't ask your friends to test your app. They like you too much to tell you the truth.**
When you build a web app, a thesis capstone, or a client site, you need real, unbiased feedback from people who don't know you. 

### The Problem
*   **Enterprise Tools are Too Expensive:** Platforms like UserTesting or Maze start at $99/month (~₱5,800+) and target corporate budgets.
*   **No Local Context:** Global panels don't understand local terms, Philippine web speeds, or local transaction methods like GCash.
*   **Unrealistic Feedback:** Friends and classmates will click through and say "looks good!" to be polite, leaving you to fail your thesis defense or get client complaints.

### The Solution: subukAn
An on-demand, async user testing platform built specifically for the Philippine tech community (students, freelancers, and indie developers).
*   **Priced for Any Project Scale:** Choose a custom participant count and a rate per tester chosen from a discrete set of tiers: ₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱300, ₱400, ₱500, up to ₱1000, ₱1100, etc. The participant count scales flexibly based on your budget and chosen rate tier—enabling high-budget projects to scale to 10 or more testers.
*   **Real Local Testers:** Recruit actual Filipino students, side-hustlers, and tech-comfort groups to test your app on their actual devices (mobile or desktop).
*   **Guaranteed Quality via Escrow:** Your budget is held securely by the platform. You review each tester's submission individually and only release payment to their **GCash** once they successfully complete your tasks.
*   **Rich, Direct Insights:** Testers follow your checklist, answer custom questions, submit screen recordings/screenshots, and provide metrics (time-on-task, ease of use, completion status).

---

### How It Works

1.  **Post Your App:** Link your live site, list 3–5 core tasks, and specify your questions.
2.  **Fund the Escrow:** Choose a budget and rate tier based on your target scale. For example, fund ₱1,000 for 10 testers at the ₱100/tester tier, or fund ₱5,000 for 10 testers at the ₱500/tester tier for complex tests. Funds are held securely in escrow by the platform.
3.  **Testers Start Instantly:** Qualified testers claim a slot, review instructions, agree to honest completion, and run the test.
4.  **Review & Release:** Listen to their voice/screen recordings, check their answers, and click **Release Payment** directly to their GCash. Unfilled slots are refunded to the platform balance or forfeited according to system rules.

## 2. Builder (Poster) Interview Script
*Target: 5–10 Filipino tech students (working on capstone/thesis), freelance web devs, or indie hackers.*

### Phase 1: Warm-up & Current Behavior (5 minutes)
1.  What project are you currently building or have you recently launched?
2.  How do you currently test if users understand how to use your site/app before launching it?
3.  How do you recruit people to try it out?
4.  What are the biggest challenges you face when trying to get feedback?

### Phase 2: Hurdles & Friction Points (5 minutes)
1.  Have you ever looked into commercial user testing tools (like Maze, UserTesting, Lyssna)? Why did you or didn't you use them?
2.  When friends or classmates test your app, how honest do you feel their feedback is? Can you share a time when a user found a bug or got stuck only *after* launch?
3.  How much time do you spend sitting next to someone watching them use your app, or manually explaining how to use it?

### Phase 3: Concept Presentation (5 minutes)
*Action: Hand them the 1-Page Concept Pitch or explain it to them in detail. Let them read it.*
1.  In your own words, how would you describe what subukAn does?
2.  What is your initial reaction to this concept? Does anything stand out immediately as useful?

### Phase 4: Pricing & Mechanic Validation (5 minutes)
1.  If you needed user feedback on your app, would you pay a custom budget for a group of testers, choosing a per-tester rate from discrete tiers (₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱300, ₱400, ₱500, up to ₱1000, ₱1100, etc.)?
    *   For a simple check with 10 testers at the ₱50/tester tier (total ₱500), does this feel accessible?
    *   For a complex, deep-dive review with 10 testers at the ₱500/tester tier (total ₱5,000) or ₱1000/tester tier (total ₱10,000), does this fit your project budget?
    *   What rate tier and participant count (e.g., 5, 10, or more) would you choose for your current project?
2.  **Escrow Mechanic:** The platform requires you to pay the budget upfront into escrow (secured via PayMongo/GCash) before the listing goes live. This ensures testers know they will be paid. How do you feel about pre-funding the test?
3.  **Rejection Mechanic:** If a tester submits a low-effort or incomplete test (e.g., they didn't follow instructions or submitted a blank video), you can reject it by selecting a reason (dropdown) and writing a 2–3 sentence explanation. Does this feel fair? Would you want a manual dispute system?
4.  **Preview Round:** Before committing a larger budget (e.g., ₱1,000+ for 10+ testers), would you value a "Preview Round" where you pay for just a single tester at your chosen tier (e.g., ₱50, ₱100, ₱200, etc.) to test your task setup first?

### Phase 5: Closing & Call to Action (3 minutes)
1.  If we did a manual pilot version of this next week (where you provide a Google Form/site link, choose a custom rate tier like ₱100/tester, and recruit a custom group of testers—even scaling up to 10 or more if your budget allows—while we handle payout manually), would you be willing to try it?
2.  Can we contact you when the pilot is ready? What is your preferred contact method (Email/Telegram/Discord)?

## 3. Tester Interview Script
*Target: 5–10 Filipino students, side-hustlers, or casual internet users looking to earn extra money.*

### Phase 1: Warm-up & Background (5 minutes)
1.  Are you currently a student, employee, freelancer, or job seeker?
2.  Have you ever participated in online side-hustles or micro-tasks (e.g., micro-surveys, CAPTCHA entry, user tests)? If yes, which ones?
3.  What payment apps do you use most frequently? Is GCash your primary digital wallet?

### Phase 2: Device & Tech Profile (5 minutes)
1.  What devices do you use most frequently to browse the web (Android phone, iPhone, Laptop, Desktop)?
2.  How would you describe your comfort level with tech (e.g., "I struggle with new apps," "I can navigate most sites," "I am a power user/student in tech")?
3.  What kind of internet connection do you usually use (Mobile Data, fiber home WiFi, public/school WiFi)?
4.  Are you comfortable installing browser extensions (like screen recorders) or uploading videos up to 100MB?

### Phase 3: Concept Presentation (5 minutes)
*Action: Explain the tester side of subukAn: "You will claim a slot, read a checklist of tasks to do on a Filipino developer's web app, record your screen while doing it, and write answers to a few short questions about what was confusing. Builders choose their rate tier from ₱50, ₱100, ₱200, up to ₱1000, ₱1100, etc. depending on complexity. Once the builder reviews it, you get paid directly to your GCash."*
1.  Does this sound like something you would be interested in doing?
2.  What do you think is the main benefit or drawback of this setup?

### Phase 4: Payout & Motivation Validation (5 minutes)
1.  We plan to offer customizable per-tester rates chosen from discrete tiers (₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱300, ₱400, ₱500, up to ₱1000, ₱1100, etc.) for a task depending on the complexity and length (e.g., 10–15 minutes of work for basic tiers, longer for higher tiers).
    *   How do you feel about these payment tiers?
    *   Would you be motivated to do more complex, specialized, or technical testing if the rate scaled up to ₱500 or ₱1000+?
    *   What is the minimum rate tier you would expect for a basic 15-minute usability test?
2.  If the payout was processed via bank transfer or Maya instead of GCash, would you still do it?

### Phase 5: Mechanics & Friction Check (5 minutes)
1.  **Agreement Modal:** Before you start a task, you must scroll to the bottom of an agreement stating you will complete the task honestly and that fake entries will be rejected. How do you feel about this screen? Does it deter you?
2.  **Timer constraint:** Once you accept a slot, you have 30 or 60 minutes to complete it, or the slot expires and is given to someone else. Does this time window feel reasonable?
3.  **Video & Screen Recording:** Are you comfortable sharing your screen and optionally your microphone/voice to point out issues, or would you only want to submit screenshots and text?

### Phase 6: Closing & Call to Action (3 minutes)
1.  We are running a manual pilot next week where builders can choose rates from ₱50 up to ₱500+ depending on the complexity of their test. Would you like to be on our tester list to participate in these custom-tiered tests?
2.  What is the best way to send you the test link (mobile number, Telegram, email)?

## 5. Synthesis & "Pivot or Proceed" Checklist

After logging all interviews, review the data and check off the following conditions before initiating the manual pilot (Validation Step 2):

### Validation Thresholds

- [ ] **Market Demand:** At least 3 of 5 builders state they have a current project that needs testing and struggle to find testers.
- [ ] **Price Point & Tiers:** At least 4 of 5 builders agree that a discrete tiered pricing system (₱50, ₱100, ₱200, ₱300, ₱400, ₱500, up to ₱1000, ₱1100, etc.) is easy to understand and provides appropriate options for different project complexities and budgets.
- [ ] **Scale Flexibility:** At least 3 of 5 builders indicate they would scale their tests to 10 or more participants if budget and project requirements dictate it.
- [ ] **Escrow Buy-In:** No builder strongly refuses the upfront escrow model once explained (or they understand the platform safety benefit).
- [ ] **Tester Supply & Tier Motivation:** At least 4 of 5 testers agree that the entry-level tiers (₱50-₱100) are acceptable for simple tests, and express interest/motivation in performing detailed or longer tests at higher tiers (₱200 to ₱1000+).
- [ ] **Technical Feasibility (Tester):** All testers have access to a device and are comfortable with the screen recording requirement.
- [ ] **GCash Confirmation:** GCash is confirmed as the essential payment channel for testers.
