# subukAn Pilot Tester Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the **subukAn Usability Testing Pilot**! As a pilot tester, your feedback is crucial in helping web app creators in the Philippines build intuitive, user-friendly applications. 

This guide provides step-by-step instructions on setting up screen recording software, receiving test tasks, conducting tests using the think-aloud protocol, and receiving your GCash payouts.

---

## 1. Overview & Expectations

* **Your Role:** Test assigned web applications, record your screen and microphone, speak your thoughts out loud as you navigate, and log your structured feedback.
* **Duration:** Each test session takes approximately **10 to 15 minutes**.
* **Reward & Payout:** You will receive a direct **GCash payment** per approved test submission (typically within 24 hours of review).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Quality Requirement:** To receive payout, your recording MUST include **clear, audible microphone sound** with continuous think-aloud commentary and a **publicly accessible video link**.

---

## 2. Step 1: Screen & Audio Recording Setup

You can use any free screen recording tool. Below are the recommended options:

### Option A: Loom (Recommended for Desktop / Chrome)
1. Install the free **Loom Chrome Extension** or Loom Desktop App from [loom.com](https://www.loom.com).
2. Create a free account or log in with Google.
3. Before recording, select:
   * **Recording Mode:** Screen Only (or Screen + Camera if preferred).
   * **Microphone:** Select your active microphone (ensure the audio volume bar moves when you speak).
4. Click **Start Recording** and test speaking: *"Mic check, testing audio."*
5. When finished, click **Finish Recording**. Loom automatically copies a shareable link to your clipboard.

### Option B: OBS Studio / Native Recorder + Google Drive (Desktop & Mobile)
1. Use **OBS Studio** (Desktop), **Windows Game Bar** (`Win + Alt + R`), **macOS QuickTime** (`Cmd + Shift + 5`), or your smartphone's built-in Screen Recorder.
2. **Crucial:** Enable **Microphone Recording** in your recorder settings.
3. Record your session and save the output video file (`.mp4` or `.webm`).
4. Upload the video file to your **Google Drive**.
5. Right-click the file in Google Drive -> **Share** -> Change Access to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
6. Copy the shareable link.

> [!TIP]
> Always verify your video share link in an **Incognito/Private Browser Window** to confirm anyone can view it without requiring a Google login request.

---

## 3. Step 2: Receiving & Claiming Tasks

1. The **Pilot Coordinator** will send available test tasks via email, Telegram, or Viber group.
2. Review the task details:
   * **Test App URL:** Target website to test.
   * **Target Tasks:** Specific workflows to perform (e.g., Sign up, Search product, Checkout).
   * **Reward Rate:** GCash amount for completion.
   * **Google Form Link:** The submission link for metrics and video URL.
3. Confirm your participation with the Pilot Coordinator to claim your slot.

---

## 4. Step 3: Executing Test Scripts (Think-Aloud Protocol)

Usability testing is not just about finishing tasks — app creators want to hear **what you are thinking** as you navigate.

### What is the "Think-Aloud Protocol"?
Speak continuously as you test. Share your immediate reactions, expectations, and any confusion:
* *"I am looking for the sign-up button... I expect it to be at the top right, but I don't see it."*
* *"This form label is confusing because I'm not sure if I should use my username or email."*
* *"The page took a few seconds to load, which made me wonder if my click registered."*

### Step-by-Step Testing Workflow
1. **Prepare Your Workspace:** Open the target test application in one browser tab and the subukAn Google Form in another.
2. **Start Screen & Mic Recording:** Perform a 5-second voice check out loud.
3. **Execute Task 1:** Follow the instructions in Section 3 of the Google Form. Note the start time on your recording counter.
4. **Execute Task 2 & Task 3:** Complete remaining workflows while continuously speaking out loud.
5. **Stop Recording:** Once all tasks are completed, stop the screen recorder and generate/save the shareable URL.

---

## 5. Step 4: Submitting Feedback & Video Link

1. Switch back to the **Google Form** tab.
2. Fill out all required fields:
   * **Pre-test Profile:** Full Name, GCash Number (`09XXXXXXXXX`), Device Type, Tech Comfort Level.
   * **Task Metrics:** Completion status, Difficulty rating (1–5 scale), Video Timestamps (`MM:SS`), and Qualitative pain points.
   * **Video Share URL:** Paste your Loom URL or Google Drive shareable link.
3. Review your answers and click **Submit**.

---

## 6. Step 5: Review & Payout Process

1. **Submission Review:** The Pilot Coordinator verifies:
   * Video opens correctly without permission errors.
   * Audio is clear and audible throughout.
   * All assigned tasks were attempted or completed.
2. **Payout Disbursement:** Once approved, your GCash payment will be sent directly to your registered GCash number within **24 hours**.
3. **Notification:** You will receive a text message / GCash reference receipt confirming the transfer.

---

## 7. FAQ & Troubleshooting

* **Q: What if I encounter a broken button or bug on the test app?**  
  * **A:** That's great feedback! Describe the bug out loud on video, log it in the form feedback section, and proceed to the next step if possible.
* **Q: My Loom link says "Private" or "Access Denied".**  
  * **A:** Open Loom, click on your video settings, and change visibility to **"Public"** or **"Anyone with the link"**.
* **Q: I forgot to turn on my microphone during recording. What should I do?**  
  * **A:** Unfortunately, recordings without audio cannot be accepted. You will need to re-record the session with audio turned ON.
* **Q: Who do I contact if I need help during testing?**  
  * **A:** Reach out to the Pilot Coordinator directly on Telegram/Viber or email `support@subukan.ph`.
